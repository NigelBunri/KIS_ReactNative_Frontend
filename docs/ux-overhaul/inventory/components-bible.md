# Inventory: `src/components/Bible/**`

**Scope:** Bible reading, Bible games (30 games + journey-map progression), 12 Pillars discipleship content, translation picker, prayer/meditation/devotions/lessons/plans panels, KCAN books/messages library.

**Count:** 58 of 58 assigned files inventoried. 0 files remain NOT YET INVENTORIED.

**Reachability:** All 58 files are reachable from the app. `src/screens/tabs/BibleScreen.tsx` is the hub — it is mounted as the "Bible" main tab (registered in `src/navigation/AppNavigator.tsx:951`, tab-bar logic referenced in root `App.tsx:613`) and switches between 11 tab keys (`read`, `daily`, `meditations`, `prayer-calendar`, `reading-planner`, `discipleship`, `lessons`, `books`, `games`, `messages`, `settings`), each rendering one top-level panel from this directory. `BibleGamesPanel.tsx` is itself a sub-hub: its `GAME_REGISTRY` lazy-imports all 30 game engines under `games/**` by `GameKey`, confirmed by a direct count (`grep -c "Game'" BibleGamesPanel.tsx` → 30/30). `games/journey/GameJourneyScreen.tsx` + `JourneyMap.tsx` are the per-game progression screen every one of the 30 games opens into before gameplay. `TwelvePillarsPanel.tsx` (mounted at the `discipleship` tab) pulls in `discipleship/DayRoadmap.tsx`, `discipleship/DiscipleshipReadAloudSheet.tsx`, and `discipleship/discipleshipStorage.ts`. One file lives a second life outside BibleScreen entirely: `BibleCourseDetailSheet.tsx` is also imported by `src/components/partners/center/PartnerCoursesSection.tsx` (general course-detail modal, not Bible-specific despite its location in this folder and its `is_bible_course` flag). No dead files were found in this scope.

**Age-inclusive design observation (inventory-stage, not yet audited):** The 30 Bible games skew toward literate-adult mechanics — crosswords, word search, spaced-repetition flashcards (Verse Vault's SM-2-lite scheduler), timed trivia, memory-pairs — rather than toward younger or lower-literacy players. Docblocks are explicit that content is drawn from raw KJV text (archaic English, e.g. "begat", "shall") with zero curation or simplification, and several games explicitly tune for *difficulty* (Flash Recall's 1.5-3s flash window, Chapter Sprint's 8s countdown) rather than accessibility. There is no reading-level toggle, no larger-text/reduced-motion game mode, and no "easy mode" distinct from the base mechanic. This is consistent with "age-inclusive" meaning "not childish" here (good — avoids a kiddie-app skin), but it also means younger players, low-literacy adults, or anyone intimidated by archaic English have no on-ramp; worth flagging explicitly at audit time rather than assuming the games are universally approachable just because they're not infantilized. `TwelvePillarsPanel`/discipleship content is denser still (12 doctrines x 6 days x MCQ tests), aimed squarely at an engaged adult learner.

## Summary of inventory-stage signals worth carrying into the audit

- **`BibleReaderPanel.tsx` (3216 lines) and `BibleCourseDetailSheet.tsx` (1515 lines)** are by far the largest/highest-complexity files in this scope — most promising targets for deep behavioral audit (offline download flows, audio recording, billing gate, video/PDF playback all live here).
- **Error handling is uneven**: only 4 of 58 files contain a `catch` block at all (`BibleMessagesPanel`, `BibleReaderPanel`, `BibleCourseDetailSheet`, `discipleshipStorage.ts`), and only 7 files use `Alert.alert`. Most of the 30 game engines have no visible error-handling path — likely fine since they're pure offline/local-data games, but worth confirming none of them can receive a malformed/empty verse partition at runtime.
- **Only `BibleReaderPanel.tsx` uses `RefreshControl`** (pull-to-refresh) in this entire scope — every other list/feed panel (Messages, Books, Meditations, Devotions, Lessons, Plans) has no pull-to-refresh, which may be an inconsistency worth flagging for the UX pass.
- **Two read-aloud sheets exist by design** (`BibleReadAloudSheet.tsx` for the main reader, `DiscipleshipReadAloudSheet.tsx` for the 12 Pillars day content) — intentionally not unified per their docblocks, but worth confirming at audit time that the two still feel like the same product.
- **`BibleCourseDetailSheet.tsx` is dual-purpose** — it is not exclusively a "Bible" component despite its folder location; it is the generic course-detail sheet also used by Partner Center's general course catalog (`PartnerCoursesSection.tsx`). Any audit changes here affect non-Bible flows too.
